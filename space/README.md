# Knesset 2026: a decision-model forecast

An interactive view of a pre-registered forecast of Israel's 27 October 2026 Knesset election.

**Panel:** two OpenRouter decision models, `cloudflare/clef` and `typesafe/jev-1.14`. No LLMs were involved.

**Questions:** 39 probability questions, asked under two conditions from a frozen evidence pack:

- without prediction-market prices;
- with Kalshi and Polymarket prices added.

## What the page shows

- **Questions:** every question, with each model's answer in both conditions and the market price where one exists.
- **Seats:** expected and full seat distributions per list, against the late-campaign poll mean.
- **Consistency:** the gap between two answers to the same quantity, asked in different forms.
- **Method and cost:** how the forecast was produced, and the cost of each request ($0.016 in total).
