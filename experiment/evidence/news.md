# 2026 Knesset election (26th Knesset): campaign news digest

Polling day is Tuesday 2026-10-27. The 25th Knesset dissolved in July 2026 at the end of its full term. All 120 seats are allocated by nationwide closed-list proportional representation, with a 3.25% threshold and the Bader-Ofer method ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).

## 1. State of the race in the final week (19–22 Oct)

**Thursday 2026-10-22**
- **2026-10-22.** The final Times of Israel poll was reported under the headline "blocs in dead heat, neither has majority if campaign promises kept" ([ToI liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-22-2026/)). A Jerusalem Post poll the same day was headlined that Winter's People of Israel and the Hendel–Zelekha list "may not cross electoral threshold" ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912482)).
- **2026-10-22.** Likud has shifted tactics. After failing to persuade Ofer Winter (People of Israel) and Moti Leitner (Charedi Public) to quit, it is now targeting their right-wing voters directly. In a long Facebook post Netanyahu urged supporters of small lists that are unlikely to pass the threshold to switch, and both he and Smotrich invoked 1992, when a split right-wing vote cost the right power ([JPost analysis](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912518)).
- **2026-10-22.** At the Rabin memorial (31st anniversary), President Herzog warned that "Words kill," noted that online threats against elected officials continue, and said Yigal Amir must never be released. Netanyahu did not attend; he has skipped the state ceremony since 2019. At Rabin Square, Yair Golan accused Netanyahu of "creating the conditions for the next Yigal Amir" ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/words-kill-herzog-pleads-for-civility-ahead-of-election-at-rabin-memorial-ceremony/)).
- **2026-10-22.** Defense Minister Israel Katz said Lapid, Bennett and Golan would be to blame for any future attack, because they cast doubt on the government's warnings of possible enemy attacks around the election. Eisenkot replied that Katz was "shifting blame in advance," Lapid called the statement a gift to Israel's enemies, and Golan called it panic. Security sources cited by Kan, Haaretz and Army Radio said the IDF had not identified a specific threat ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/katz-says-opposition-leaders-to-blame-for-any-future-attacks-on-israel/)).
- **2026-10-22.** Channel 13 reported that IDF Chief of Staff Eyal Zamir said privately that he sees no operational need to strike Iran before the vote ([ToI liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/report-idf-chief-said-he-doesnt-see-operational-need-to-strike-iran-ahead-of-election/)). This followed a Kan report on 2026-10-21 that security officials had warned a pre-election strike could be seen as political interference. Lapid told Kan on 2026-10-22 that there was no reason to strike before the vote and accused Netanyahu of artificially escalating tensions ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912507)).
- **2026-10-22.** A three-judge High Court panel ruled 2–1 that party-appointed polling-station officials may not count voters digitally and transmit the counts to their parties in real time. Likud had requested the arrangement and the acting CEC director had approved it; Israel Democracy Guard petitioned against it ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/high-court-rules-polling-station-officials-cant-transmit-voter-data-to-parties-on-election-day/)).
- **2026-10-22.** US Vice President JD Vance said "I'm not endorsing Bibi Netanyahu" and that he would endorse no more foreign candidates. He added that Trump's endorsement is what matters ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912474)).
- **2026-10-22.** Smotrich claimed that in 2021 Netanyahu agreed to a coalition arrangement with Ra'am and that he himself blocked it. He did not rule out sitting with Eisenkot, but rejected any boycott of his right-wing partners ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912494)).
- **2026-10-22.** Haaretz reported that IDF and Shin Bet intelligence presented to US officers says Hamas does not intend to disarm and is rebuilding ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/report-israel-tells-us-hamas-doesnt-plan-to-disarm-and-is-rebuilding-its-forces/)).

**19–21 Oct**

He said "the next defense minister should be from Likud," which rebuffs Ben-Gvir's demand for that post. He called his trial "collapsed" and said it should be ended ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912462)).

In September, salt shakers thrown at him in Bnei Brak hit two staff members, and a suspect has been indicted. - **2026-10-19.** A rock thrown in Turmus Ayya lightly injured People of Israel candidate Fleur Hassan-Nahoum during a pro-settlement tour. The same day, a Democrats billboard of Golan was vandalized with threatening graffiti ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election); [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/palestinians-attack-pro-settlement-group-including-right-wing-politician-her-party-says/)).
- **2026-10-18.** A former Likud campaign technology official submitted a sworn affidavit to the High Court and the attorney general. It alleges that Likud's "Victory 26" election-day app can be used to monitor polling stations covertly, getting around court rulings ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ex-likud-official-warns-party-app-could-be-used-to-illegally-monitor-polling-stations/)).
- **2026-10-18.** On Truth Social, Trump gave a "Complete and Total Endorsement of Bibi Netanyahu." He wrote that if Israel chose anyone else, "America will have to do this work ourselves." Three days earlier, in a Newsmax interview, he had declined to endorse anyone. Netanyahu thanked him; opposition leaders said Israelis would choose their own leaders ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/trump-offers-warrior-netanyahu-complete-and-total-endorsement-for-reelection/); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912474)).

## 2. Alliances, mergers, withdrawals, endorsements

**Main lists and how they formed**
- **Together / B'Yachad (Bennett–Lapid).** Naftali Bennett and Yair Lapid (Yesh Atid) agreed in April 2026 to run on a joint list headed by Bennett, with Lapid at No. 2 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election); [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/katz-says-opposition-leaders-to-blame-for-any-future-attacks-on-israel/)).
- **Yashar (Gadi Eisenkot).** Founded in September 2025. On 2026-09-05 Hili Tropper left the Zionist Home–Reservists alliance to join it. Outlets describe Eisenkot as the frontrunner and opposition leader ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election); [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/katz-says-opposition-leaders-to-blame-for-any-future-attacks-on-israel/)).
- **Reservists–Economic Party (Yoaz Hendel + Yaron Zelekha's New Economic Party).** The alliance formed after the Zionist Home–Reservists alliance split on 2026-09-05. Outlets report it as polling near or below the threshold ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912482)).
- **Amcha Yisrael / People of Israel (Ofer Winter).** A new right-wing party with Yoseph Haddad at No. 2 and Fleur Hassan-Nahoum at No. 5. It has endorsed Netanyahu for prime minister, and Haddad said that endorsement stands ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912440)). Outlets report it polling below or just above the threshold.
- **The Democrats (Yair Golan).** Labor and Meretz merged in 2024; a digital primary was held 2026-07-20 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
- **Joint List (Hadash, Ta'al, Balad), chaired by Yousef Jabareen.** Ra'am (Mansour Abbas) runs separately and has put a Jewish former police official, Yoav Segalovitz, at No. 2 ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/turnout-among-israels-arab-voters-could-make-or-break-hopes-to-unseat-netanyahu/); [JPost](https://jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-908431)).
- **Otzma Yehudit (Ben-Gvir).** Tally Gotliv left Likud and took the No. 2 slot (2026; exact date not confirmed) ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h18003pbkzl)).
- **Likud.** Its primary was held 2026-08-17. A Likud–New Hope merger was signed in 2025 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
- **Charedi Public party (Moti Leitner).** A Haredi party with comparatively moderate positions on enlistment and core curriculum. No poll has shown it entering the Knesset ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/pm-said-to-meet-head-of-charedi-public-party-in-last-ditch-effort-to-get-him-to-quit-race/)).

**Withdrawals and endorsements**
- **2026-09-04.** Gilad Erdan's Unity dropped out ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
- **2026-10-02.** Balad chief Sami Abu Shehadeh withdrew his candidacy after Supreme Court President Amit indicated that a majority of the bench would bar him ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/supreme-court-unanimously-overturns-election-panels-ban-on-arab-parties/)).
- **2026-10-13.** People of Israel's No. 8, Davidi Ben-Zion, quit, saying he would not risk wasting right-wing votes. The party blamed a harassment campaign ([INN](https://www.israelnationalnews.com/news/434562); [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/winter-vows-to-stay-in-race-despite-pressure-campaign-targeting-his-party/)).
- **2026-10-19.** **Benny Gantz withdrew Blue and White**, eight days before the vote, after polls repeatedly showed it below the threshold. He said "the responsibility is mine" and that he would "continue to fight for unity," and he called for a broad unity government. **No outlet reviewed reports that he endorsed any party or candidate.** The list stays on the ballot, so votes cast for it are wasted. Eisenkot praised the decision; Hendel and Winter pitched for Blue and White voters; Gideon Sa'ar urged small right-wing lists to follow suit ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/blue-and-whites-benny-gantz-pulls-out-of-race-sealing-precipitous-political-fall/); [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/s1sdbqm2fe); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/19/benny-gantz-drops-out-of-israels-election-race)).
- **2026-10-20.** Netanyahu met Leitner in Petah Tikva to press him to quit. Reported offers included a post as special adviser on Haredi affairs. Leitner has said he lacks the "moral right" to withdraw and is staying in ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/pm-said-to-meet-head-of-charedi-public-party-in-last-ditch-effort-to-get-him-to-quit-race/)).
- **2026-10-21.** Smotrich offered Winter a Defense Ministry post from RZP's allocation, plus directorates for Gaza "voluntary emigration" and Haredi enlistment, in exchange for withdrawing. Winter refused: "We will not surrender." Netanyahu told Winter publicly that he passes the threshold in no poll and urged him to quit to "save the right" ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912440)). Netanyahu and Smotrich also met Winter's rabbi, Eli Sadan, to apply pressure ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912402)).
- **2026-10-21.** Nissim Louk, father of 7 October victim Shani Louk, quit the Orot Hashachar list and endorsed Winter's party ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912345)).
- **As of 2026-10-22**, People of Israel and Charedi Public both say they are staying in the race ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/people-of-israel-charedi-public-vow-to-stay-in-race-despite-pressure-from-pm-and-allies/)).

## 3. Surplus-vote agreements (deadline 2026-10-16)

An agreement takes effect only if both partner lists cross the threshold.
1. **Together (Bennett–Lapid) + Yisrael Beytenu**, signed 2026-09-10 ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-911320); [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
2. **Yashar + The Democrats**, signed 2026-09-10 (same sources).
3. **Hadash-Ta'al (Joint List) + Ra'am**, signed 2026-09-11. In 2022 the Arab parties had no such agreement ([JPost](https://jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-908431)).
4. **Likud + Religious Zionist Party** (listed by Wikipedia as RZP–Zehut), signed 2026-10-06. Otzma Yehudit protested and said Likud had refused its own offer ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog_entry/likud-and-religious-zionism-sign-surplus-vote-agreement/)).
5. **Shas + United Torah Judaism**, announced 2026-10-11. The two parties also pledged to cooperate in the next Knesset on Torah study, the status of yeshiva students and religious education ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-911320)).

**Lists with no agreement reported:** Otzma Yehudit, People of Israel and Reservists–Economic. Ben-Gvir refused a deal with Winter on 2026-10-13 ("Winter is Bennett II"), and Hendel said he would not sign with Ben-Gvir ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ben-gvir-rules-out-surplus-vote-deal-with-winter-calls-him-bennett-2/); [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/s1ql002sjge)).

## 4. CEC and Supreme Court rulings

- **2026-09-14.** The Democrats petitioned the CEC to disqualify Otzma Yehudit, and Otzma counter-petitioned against the Democrats ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
- **2026-09-17.** The High Court barred parties from collecting real-time turnout and voter-identity data at polling stations ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
- **2026-09-23.** **The CEC barred Ra'am (18–5), the Joint List, MK Ofer Cassif and Sami Abu Shehadeh.** The petitioners were Likud, Otzma Yehudit and the Choosing Life forum. They alleged support for armed struggle and rejection of Israel as a Jewish and democratic state. Attorney General Baharav-Miara opposed barring Ra'am. The committee chair's vote broke with precedent ([i24](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israeli-elections/artc-israel-s-election-panel-bars-ra-am-and-joint-list-supreme-court-fight-looms); [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/24/israel-moves-to-bar-both-main-arab-lists-from-october-election)).
- **2026-09-24.** The CEC voted against disqualifying the Democrats (14–2), Otzma Yehudit (19–10) and RZP (21–3) ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
- **2026-10-02.** **The Supreme Court unanimously overturned the bans on Ra'am and the Joint List, and ruled 7–2 that Cassif may run.** Abu Shehadeh had withdrawn first. His case concerned an article of 8 October 2023 that he says he regrets. Balad says he remains party chief ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/supreme-court-unanimously-overturns-election-panels-ban-on-arab-parties/); [Al Jazeera](https://aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/2/israels-supreme-court-overturns-election-panel-ban-on-arab-parties)).
- **2026-10-04.** The Supreme Court rejected the petitions against Otzma Yehudit, Ben-Gvir and Gotliv ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)).
- **2026-10-22.** The ruling on real-time voter counts by party officials (see §1).

## 5. Coalition-formation signals

- **Netanyahu (Likud), 2026-10-21.** Wants his current partners back, but under Likud's policy. His "first principle" is "no Palestinian state, period." The defense minister should come from Likud. Asked about a unity government with Eisenkot, he did not answer directly ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912462)). Earlier he assured Ben-Gvir that his next government would rest on the current coalition (2026, exact date unconfirmed) ([Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h18003pbkzl)). A JPost analysis on 2026-10-22 said analysts think he may consider a unity government without Ben-Gvir; this is analysis, not a statement ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912518)).
- **Ben-Gvir (Otzma).** Wants the Defense Ministry for himself and the Justice Ministry for Gotliv. He campaigns so that "Netanyahu cannot form a government without him" ([i24](https://www.i24news.tv/en/news/israel-election-s-2026/artc-ben-gvir-for-defense-minister-gotliv-for-justice-minister-otzma-yehudit-publishes-coalition-demands-for-the-next-government); [Ynet](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h18003pbkzl)).
- **Smotrich (RZP), 2026-10-22.** Open to Eisenkot or Golan joining, but rejects any boycott of Ben-Gvir ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912494)).
- **Haredi parties, 2026-10-20.** Shas and UTJ say they will not join a new coalition until a draft-exemption law is fully passed ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/netanyahu-comes-out-against-arrest-of-haredi-draft-dodgers-as-42-detained-at-airport/)).
- **Eisenkot (Yashar), 2026-10-21.** "I will not serve under Netanyahu, not even in a rotation agreement" ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912401)). He rules out partnering with Arab parties because they refuse to label Hamas a terrorist organization ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/turnout-among-israels-arab-voters-could-make-or-break-hopes-to-unseat-netanyahu/)). Reported on 2026-10-15: he would lead a minority government if neither bloc reaches 61 ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-911855)).
- **Bennett (Together), 2026-10-11/12.** Suggested Netanyahu could join if he accepted the opposition's core principles, then retracted: "We will not sit with him in any formation." Eisenkot, Golan and Liberman rejected the idea; Gantz backed it ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/bennett-suggests-netanyahu-could-be-part-of-future-government-quickly-backtracks/)). On 2026-10-21 he said he wants a coalition of Zionist parties only and will accept voters' choice on the premiership ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912343)).
- **Liberman (Yisrael Beytenu), 2026-10-15.** Will not sit with Netanyahu "under any circumstances," but "Likud without Netanyahu can be a significant partner." His formula is "No Ra'am, no Joint List, no Shas, and no United Torah Judaism," and he will not support a minority government, even a transitional one ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-911855)).
- **Golan (Democrats), 2026-10-20.** The only opposition leader openly backing Ra'am's inclusion in the coalition. He rules out Haredi parties that were in the 7 October government ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912335)).
- **Hendel (Reservists–Economic), 2026-10-22.** Will join only a coalition of Zionist parties, and would sit with Netanyahu, Eisenkot or Bennett. He will not sit with Haredi parties, and says he aims to "hold the deciding vote" ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912595)).
- **Winter (People of Israel), 2026-10-21.** Will back only a government that drafts Haredim. He said he will stay in Netanyahu's bloc if a conscription law passes ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912402)).
- **Arab parties.** Abbas (Ra'am) says Arab voters "can ensure the formation of a new government of change" and sees room to manoeuvre after the vote. Ahmad Tibi (Joint List) offered to act as a "preventive block" supporting a government from outside, including on a vote to set up a 7 October inquiry ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/turnout-among-israels-arab-voters-could-make-or-break-hopes-to-unseat-netanyahu/)). Jabareen set out conditions for backing a minority government (reported 2026-10-15) ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-911855)).

## 6. Campaign-defining issues and events

**Iran**
- **Background.** The US and Israel went to war with Iran on 2026-02-28. A Pakistan-brokered ceasefire ran from 8 April to 8 July, when fighting resumed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Iran_war_ceasefire)). As of late October, reporting describes a pause in fighting with stalled nuclear talks. Iran is blocking the Strait of Hormuz and the US is blockading Iranian ports ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/netanyahu-planned-attack-on-iran-earlier-this-month-but-trump-opposed-it-report/)).
- **2026-10-09.** Trump said the US would not attack Iran before the US midterms on 2026-11-03.
- **2026-10-21.** The FT reported that Netanyahu had planned strikes for the weekend of 2026-10-10 and that Trump opposed them. The NYT reported that the US and Israel have drafted plans for renewed strikes after the midterms (same ToI source; [ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/us-israel-said-to-draft-plans-for-resumed-strikes-on-iran-after-midterm-elections/)).

**Gaza**
- **2026-08-03.** The Board of Peace said Israel will not withdraw beyond the "Yellow Line" until Hamas disarms ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/4/board-of-peace-says-no-israeli-withdrawal-from-gaza-before-hamas-disarms)).
- **2026-10-18.** Bennett pledged to keep the Yellow Line, declare Qatar an enemy state and dismantle Hamas, if necessary by force ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912343)).

**Lebanon**
- **Background.** War resumed 2026-03-02. A truce has been in force since 2026-04-16, with continued Israeli strikes. A US framework was announced 2026-06-26, and Israel handed over parts of its buffer zone in July ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Lebanon_war)). No major October Lebanon escalation was found in the reporting reviewed.

**West Bank**
- **Week of 19–22 Oct.** Settler attacks during the olive harvest were reported repeatedly. Netanyahu said on 2026-10-21, "I don't want vigilantism" ([ToI liveblog](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-october-22-2026/); [JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912462)).

**The 7 October inquiry**
- All main opposition parties pledge a state commission of inquiry, and Eisenkot says he would set one up "immediately." Netanyahu favors a politically appointed panel. The attorney general backed deferring the issue until after the election ([Irish Times, 2026-10-07](https://www.irishtimes.com/world/2026/10/07/netanyahu-faces-fresh-scrutiny-over-pre-october-7th-warnings/)).

**Haredi draft**
- **Early October.** The attorney general ordered the IDF to resume arresting draft evaders. The AG's office now says more than 67,000 Haredi men are evading the draft ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ag-over-67000-haredi-men-are-dodging-draft-high-court-must-weigh-more-sanctions/)).

Netanyahu called the arrests "a deliberate attempt to harm the process of drafting Haredim without coercion" and said his coalition was one vote short of passing a draft law. Eisenkot and Bennett condemned him. Shas accused the attorney general of election interference, and UTJ's Yaakov Asher alleged voter intimidation ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/netanyahu-comes-out-against-arrest-of-haredi-draft-dodgers-as-42-detained-at-airport/)).

He acknowledged that he had not approved every police step and had not reviewed all 315 alleged media-bribery examples in Case 4000. Likud demanded that the trial be halted, and Netanyahu said "the truth is coming out." The defense sought further sessions on 18–22 Oct ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/ex-ag-admits-he-didnt-approve-all-police-action-or-review-full-netanyahu-indictment/)).

- **"Qatargate."** Netanyahu adviser Jonatan Urich is a central suspect and denies wrongdoing. Netanyahu calls the affair "a hoax" ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912462)).

## 7. Turnout-relevant factors

- **Holiday and hours.** Under Basic Law: The Knesset, election day is a paid statutory day of rest, with exceptions for some transport and public services. The IDF continues normal operations. More than 12,000 polling stations will open, mostly from 07:00 to 22:00 ([Israel Chamber of Commerce](https://www.chamber.org.il/serviceslobby/legal/1856/164171/); [Maariv](https://www.maariv.co.il/news/politics/article-1357269)).
- **Free transport.** Intercity buses on routes over 15 km and all Israel Railways trains are free from 20:00 on 2026-10-26 until service ends on election day ([JPost](https://jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-909148)).
- **Soldiers.** Voting began 2026-10-20/21 at more than 850 military polling stations, "subject to operational requirements." Zamir ordered a leave restriction for all units from 2026-10-22, and forces were reinforced, especially in the West Bank ([JPost](https://www.jpost.com/israel-election-2026/article-912376)).
- **Voters abroad.** Only diplomatic staff can vote abroad; they voted on 15–16 Oct. Other expatriates must travel to Israel. On 2026-09-02 the High Court dismissed a petition by about 100 expatriates removed from the rolls ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Israeli_legislative_election)). Israelis flying in from the US are organized through networks built during the judicial-overhaul protests ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/israelis-flying-from-us-to-vote-leaning-on-network-built-during-judicial-overhaul-protests/)).
- **Arab sector.** Ra'am and the Joint List are running get-out-the-vote drives aimed at anger over crime and over the government. Likud and RZP pledged nearly 2,000 activists at polling stations in Arab communities, citing unsubstantiated fraud claims, which raised intimidation concerns. Jabareen accused right-wing figures of trying to intimidate Arab voters ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/turnout-among-israels-arab-voters-could-make-or-break-hopes-to-unseat-netanyahu/)).
- **Security.** The government has warned of possible enemy attacks around election day, while security sources cited by Kan, Haaretz and Army Radio report no specific threat ([ToI](https://www.timesofisrael.com/katz-says-opposition-leaders-to-blame-for-any-future-attacks-on-israel/)).
